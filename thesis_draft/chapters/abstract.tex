\chapter*{ABSTRACT}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{ABSTRACT}

\textbf{Background:} Automated brain tumor detection from MRI scans is critical for efficient screening and treatment planning. While deep learning models achieve high accuracy, their black-box nature limits clinical adoption. This thesis addresses two key questions: (1) How do CNN architectures (ResNet-50, EfficientNet-B2) compare to Vision Transformers (DeiT-Base) for slice-level tumor detection? (2) Which architecture provides superior explainability for clinical trust?

\textbf{Methods: We trained three architectures on the BraTS 2021 dataset (1,251 patients, 877 training / 187 validation / 187 test) using three MRI modalities (T1ce, T2, FLAIR) as input channels. Patient-level splitting prevented data leakage. Training employed two-stage transfer learning (5 epochs frozen, 5 epochs fine-tuned) with evaluation metrics (accuracy, precision, recall, F1). Grad-CAM visualization provided interpretability.}

\textbf{Results:} ResNet-50 achieved the highest quantitative performance (94.69\% test accuracy, 93.72\% F1), followed by EfficientNet-B2 (93.67\% accuracy, 92.70\% F1) and DeiT-Base (93.46\% accuracy, 92.35\% F1). However, qualitative explainability analysis revealed that DeiT-Base produces cleaner, more focused attention maps than EfficientNet-B2 and ResNet-50, whose gradients are noisier. Analysis of nine representative samples across all three models indicates that the Vision Transformer architecture of DeiT-Base produces higher-quality features for clinical interpretation.

\textbf{Conclusion: While ResNet-50 achieves marginally higher accuracy and EfficientNet-B2 offers a good balance of performance (93.67\%), DeiT-Base demonstrates superior explainability. Using three MRI modalities (T1ce, T2, FLAIR) as input channels matches the format expected by ImageNet-pretrained models. This work shows that architectural choice affects interpretability: DeiT produces cleaner attention maps than ResNet and EfficientNet.}

\textbf{Keywords:} Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Brain Tumor Detection, Explainable AI, Grad-CAM, Transfer Learning, CNN vs Transformer, EfficientNet, ResNet, DeiT, MRI Classification
